How to Use Split PDF

ToolNest provides two distinct split modes depending on how you want to extract your pages:

Mode 1

Extract Specific Ranges

Extract only your selected pages or sections and compile them into a single new PDF document.

1

Upload PDF: Drag & drop your PDF file or click the upload box.

2

Define Ranges: Keep "Specific Ranges" selected and enter page numbers or ranges (e.g. 1-3, 5, 8-10).

3

Download Combined PDF: Click "Split PDF" to instantly download a single PDF containing only your chosen pages.

Mode 2

Extract All Pages

Separate every single page of your document into its own individual PDF file (perfect for splitting scanned contracts or invoice batches).

1

Upload PDF: Choose or drop your multi-page PDF document.

2

Choose All Pages: Click "Extract All Pages" (no page range typing required).

3

Download ZIP Archive: Click "Split PDF" to receive a ZIP file containing every page neatly numbered as separate PDFs.
